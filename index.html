Dismantling the STEM gender gap from the ground up.

"I went into Spring Session ready to build wooden bridges, and now I’m ready to keep building spaces with my voice."

As an aspiring engineer, finding myself as the only girl in a 15-person structural design workshop was a turning point. Experiencing how easily female voices are minimized in STEM environments sparked a core journey into advocacy, education equity, and visual storytelling.

For my Junior Workshop project, I designed a curriculum and wrote an original children's book to rewrite the technology narrative for young learners—partnering directly with third-graders to co-create an inclusive visual world where girls can engineer, test, fail, and succeed.
